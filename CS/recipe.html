Instant Cup Ramen

I chose Instant Cup Ramen, because it is super easy to make comfort food, that is very convienent to eat during short breaks, on trips, as well as just ocasionally when you are craving it. Instant ramen has been a big part of my life, in fact it has such a impact that I now critque food whether its better or worse than instant ramen. This is one of only 3 recipes that I actually know, and it is the first recipe that I ever learned.

Ingredients

  • instant ramen cup
  • 2 cups of water
  • chopped scallion
  • egg
  • rice
  • korean bean sprout

Steps

  1. heat up water to 100 celsius
  2. Open ramen cup, pour content of sauce packet into ramen cup
  3. pour water into ramen cup
  4. drop in egg, scallion, and bean sprouts
  5. wait 3 minutes then eat the ramen noodles
  6. after finishing ramen noodles, put rice in leftover soup and eat.
