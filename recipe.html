PB&J Sandwich

Ingredients, Steps, Why This Recipe, Fun Fact, About Me

Ingredients

  • 1 Jar of Peanut Butter
  • 2 Packet of Strawberry Jam or 1 Jar of Strawberry Jam
  • 2 pieces of whole wheat bread

Steps

  1. Take your two pieces of bread and turn them into toast
  2. Then open your jar of peanut butter and then using a table knife take a chunk full
  3. Put your chunk full of peanut butter on one of your toast then spread evenly using the table knife
  4. Next grab your 2 packets of jam open 1 and use your knife to transfer the contents onto the peanut butter spread
  5. spread the jam evenly across the peanut butter spread
  6. grab your other packet and put about half of it on top of what you already have on the bread.
  7. after confirming everything is spread evenly, put the second piece of toast on top of the side with the peanut butter and jam
  8. bon apetite

Why This Recipe

I chose the PB&J sandwich because it was the first thing I ever learned how to make. I remember the magical day when my parents first let me into the kitchen, a previously forbidden zone full of sharp dangerous objects, and let me make one for lunch.

Fun Fact

I used to live in Edgewater near a Trader Joe's, so thats where my parents often bought groceries, and where the ingredients for my first PB&J came from.
