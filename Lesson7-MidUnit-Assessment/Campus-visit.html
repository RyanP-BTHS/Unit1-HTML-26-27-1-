9th Grade College Visit: Montclair State University

On Thursday, November 14, all 9th graders will spend the day at Montclair State University. We leave at 8:15 AM and return by 4:30 PM. Read this page before Wednesday so you know what to bring.

What to Bring

Pack light. Everything below should fit in one backpack.

  • Reusable water bottle
  • Packed lunch (no glass containers)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Extra layers
    • Light jacket
    • Umbrella
  • Notebook and pen

The Day's Schedule

Times are approximate. Listen for your group leader.

  1. 8:15 AM — Meet in the east parking lot
  2. 8:30 AM — Buses depart
  3. 9:45 AM — Campus tour with student guides
  4. 12:00 PM — Lunch in the student center
  5. 1:30 PM — Admissions information session
  6. 3:15 PM — Load buses for the trip home
